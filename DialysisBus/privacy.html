Site policy

Privacy

This Phase 1 demonstration is a static, read-only site. It has no user accounts, patient information, analytics tracker, advertising tracker, or application database.

Location and search

Location access occurs only after you select “Use current location” and approve the browser prompt. The site uses the result to move the map and does not save or transmit it to an application server. Searches that match a bundled demonstration city, ZIP code, address, or facility remain in the browser. Other searches are sent directly from your browser to Google Maps services for the active interaction.

Browser storage

Shared analysis state is stored in the page URL. If a local user enters a Google Maps browser key, the key may be retained in that browser’s local storage until “Forget key” is selected. The GitHub Pages demonstration uses its published browser key instead.

Third-party services

The basemap and location search use Google Maps Platform and are subject to Google’s own privacy terms. The included facility and transit records are fictional demonstration fixtures.

Feedback contact

Privacy feedback may be submitted through the repository’s GitHub issue tracker. Do not include patient or other sensitive information.
