About the NDEC Learning Resource Index

The Neurology Digital Education Collaborative (NDEC) maintains this directory of digital resources for neurology education and clinical practice.

It is intended for medical students, residents, fellows, faculty, and practicing neurologists. Resources can be filtered by patient population, creator, learning level, subspecialty, format, review status, and access. Each listing opens the original resource from its publisher or creator.

Learning levels

Levels describe what a resource enables someone to do—not a learner’s training rank.

  1. 01 Neurology ReadinessRecognize neurologic emergencies, perform an initial assessment, begin stabilization, and know when to escalate.
  2. 02 General Neurology EssentialsEvaluate common complaints and conditions using diagnostic criteria, introductory testing, and first-line treatment.
  3. 03 Core Neurology PracticeApply full clinical reasoning and management, including treatment selection and interpretation of neuroimaging and neurophysiology.
  4. 04 Advanced & Subspecialty PracticeAddress complex, uncommon, or nuanced material requiring substantial prior knowledge.

Classification is based on the learning objective and assumed prior knowledge—not the topic alone.

NDEC Peer ReviewedThis label is used only for resources that have completed NDEC’s formal review process.
